# TerraProof MRV benchmark

Generated by `npx tsx benchmark/run.ts` on 2026-10-27. Methodology v1.

**Ground truth:** Hansen Global Forest Change v1.11 (UMD / Google, 30 m): a site is *cleared* if ≥ 50 % of a ~390 m window (≈ 15 ha) lost its trees in one year (2022 or 2023) after being ≥ 50 % tree-covered; it is *intact* if tree cover was ≥ 70 % in 2000 and no pixel was lost through 2023. Sites are sampled at random (seeded) in the southern Amazon tile (Mato Grosso). Two West-African tiles (Benin / Togo / Nigeria and Côte d'Ivoire / Liberia / Ghana) were also scanned, but 600+ random forest-belt rows yielded no window meeting the strict criteria (the forest there is too fragmented at the scale of ~15 ha), so **this benchmark does not validate TerraProof in Benin or West Africa**.

**Protocol:** each site is evaluated as of 31 December of its loss year (so the reference scenes one year earlier are before the clearing and the latest scenes after). Half of the sites of each class calibrate the two thresholds (NDVI floor 0.25, NDMI floor 0.08, moisture-only freeze at −30%); **all numbers below are on the held-out half**. An "uncertain" decision (no clear recent scene) is counted as an abstention, not as a success.

Sites: 28 total, 28 with usable Sentinel-2 data; held-out test half: 7 cleared + 7 intact.

## Honest limits
- Hansen labels are 30 m and annual; a few "cleared" windows may be partly regrowth, logging or a fire scar rather than conversion to cropland.
- Sentinel-2 cannot see through persistent cloud: in the rainy tropics many sites abstain ("uncertain"). Abstaining is the right behaviour, but it lowers coverage.
- This tests *presence of standing vegetation and its loss*. It does **not** validate the carbon quantity: the credit formula remains a proxy (area × (NDVI − 0.15) × 60 tCO2e).
- Calibration and test halves come from the same sampling frame; a different biome (dry savanna, mangrove) may need its own thresholds.
- Few sites per class: see the intervals.
